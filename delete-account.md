MediMate - Account and Data Deletion Request

At MediMate, we give you full control over your data. You can request the deletion of your entire account or specific data (such as medicines and reminders) without deleting your account.

1. How to request Full Account Deletion:
To request the permanent deletion of your MediMate account and all associated data, please follow the steps below:

Send an email to softcodes18@gmail.com from your registered email address.

Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".

In the email body, mention your registered email ID and username.

What data will be deleted:

Your account information (Name, Email)

All health data (Medicines, Reminders, Schedules)

Subscription history (if any)

What data will be kept:

We do not keep any personal data after deletion. However, basic transaction logs may be retained for legal and tax compliance purposes for up to 90 days.

2. How to request Partial Data Deletion (Without deleting your account):
You can delete specific data while keeping your account active in two ways:

Within the app: Open the MediMate app, go to the "Medicines" or "Reminders" section, swipe left on any item, and tap "Delete". You can also remove family members from the "Family" section.

Via email: Send an email to softcodes18@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Mention exactly what data you want deleted (e.g., "Please delete all my medicine history but keep my account active").

What data will be deleted (Partial Deletion):

Medicine schedules, reminders, and adherence history.

Family member connections and shared data.

What data will be kept (Partial Deletion):

Your basic account information (Name and Email) will be kept active so you can continue using the app.

Timeframe for all requests:
Your account and/or requested data will be permanently deleted within 7 working days of receiving your request.
